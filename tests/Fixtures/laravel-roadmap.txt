Laravel Six Month Roadmap

Month 1: Initial release planning and scaffolding improvements.

Month 2: Frontend tooling updates and starter kit refinements.

Month 3: Improved queue diagnostics and tracing hooks.

Month 4: Testing helpers and parallel runner enhancements.

Month 5: Observability upgrades and first-party metrics adapters.

Month 6: Official Valkey integration as a drop-in alternative to Redis
for caching, queues, and rate limiting. Valkey is the preferred choice
going forward due to its permissive open source license, and the core
team will ship first-class drivers for it during this milestone.
